Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

1931 – 2015

A Tribute to an Inspiring Visionary

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists, teachers, authors, and the 11th President of India. He inspired millions of young people to dream big and work hard to achieve their goals.

About Him

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Dr. Kalam dedicated his life to science and the development of India. He played a major role in India's space and missile development programs.

Major Achievements

🚀 Scientist

Made significant contributions to India's missile and space programs.

🇮🇳 President

Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

📚 Author

Inspired young minds through books such as Wings of Fire.
