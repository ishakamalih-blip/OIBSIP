ABOUT HER

A Life of Courage & Compassion

Savitribai Phule was one of India's most important social reformers and educators. She played a pioneering role in opening educational opportunities for girls and marginalized communities.

Along with Jyotirao Phule, she worked tirelessly against social discrimination, caste inequality and injustice.

Her life represents courage, education, equality and the power of creating change through knowledge.

HER JOURNEY

A Journey That Changed India

Important milestones from the life of Savitribai Phule.

1831

Birth

Savitribai Phule was born on 3 January 1831 in Naigaon, Maharashtra.

1840

Marriage & Education

She married Jyotirao Phule and later received education and teacher training.

1848

First Girls' School

Savitribai and Jyotirao Phule started a pioneering school for girls at Bhide Wada in Pune.

1850s

Education for All

She continued working for the education of girls and children from marginalized communities.

1873

Social Reform

She became an important figure in the Satyashodhak Samaj and the movement for social equality.

1897

Her Final Service

She served patients during the plague epidemic and passed away on 10 March 1897.
