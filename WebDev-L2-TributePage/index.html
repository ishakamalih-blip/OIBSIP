ABOUT HER

The woman who changed history.

Savitribai Phule became one of the most important pioneers of women's education and social reform in India.

Savitribai Phule was born on 3 January 1831 in Naigaon, Maharashtra.

At a time when education for girls was strongly restricted, Savitribai learned to read and write and later became a teacher.

Along with her husband, Jyotirao Phule, she worked to provide education to girls and marginalized communities.

In 1848, they established a girls' school at Bhide Wada in Pune. This became an important milestone in the history of women's education in India.

Savitribai also worked against caste discrimination, child marriage, social inequality and injustice against widows.
